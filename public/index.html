Add presents from your phone

Android (Chrome): open this site, tap the menu (three dots) then Install app. After that, on any shop page tap Share and choose Ffi's Presents.

iPhone: in Safari tap Share then Add to Home Screen. To add from a shop page, make a Shortcut: open the Shortcuts app, tap +, add the action Open URLs, and set its address to the text below followed by the Shortcut Input. In the shortcut's settings turn on Show in Share Sheet.

On a computer: drag this button to your bookmarks bar, then click it on any shop page: Add to Ffi's Presents
